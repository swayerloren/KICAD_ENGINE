# Global Unverified Claims

Status: `ACTIVE_GLOBAL_MEMORY`

Reusable record of categories of claims that must stay unverified until evidence exists.

## Claim Categories

- Exact footprint correctness.
- Connector orientation.
- Pinout mapping.
- Datasheet electrical limits.
- Package dimensions.
- Fab-house-specific manufacturing rules.
- ERC/DRC pass status.

## Required Action

Use `UNVERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`, or `REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW` until evidence is recorded.
## 2026-05-07 Current-State Claims Require Maintenance Evidence

Status: `ACTIVE_EVIDENCE`

Any claim that a project is "current", "latest", "ready", "passed", "production-ready", or allowed to proceed is unverified unless backed by current evidence files and phase-gate/memory-maintenance outputs.
