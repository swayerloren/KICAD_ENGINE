# Assembly Notes Rules

Status: `ACTIVE_RULES`

## Purpose

Assembly notes capture human-readable manufacturing instructions that BOM and PNP files cannot fully express.

## Common Notes

- DNP parts.
- Hand-soldered parts.
- Connector orientation.
- Polarity orientation.
- Mechanical hardware.
- Special cleaning or no-clean requirements.
- Programming or test steps.

## Rule

Do not write assembly notes as final instructions unless the human has reviewed them. Draft notes should be labeled `NOT_FINAL`.
