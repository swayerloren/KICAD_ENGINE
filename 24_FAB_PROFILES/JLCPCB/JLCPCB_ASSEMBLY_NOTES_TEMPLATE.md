## General Notes

- Assemble according to uploaded Gerbers, BOM, and CPL.
- Verify all component orientations before production.
- Contact customer before substitutions.

## Critical Orientation Notes

### Barrel Jack

- Confirm female barrel opening faces the correct off-board edge.
- Confirm rear solder-leg side matches the footprint.
- Do not trust CPL rotation alone.

### USB-C

- Confirm receptacle mouth faces the board edge.
- Confirm shield tabs and fine-pitch pins align with footprint.

### ICs

- Confirm pin 1 orientation.

### Diodes / LEDs

- Confirm cathode/anode orientation.

### Polarized Capacitors

- Confirm polarity before assembly.
