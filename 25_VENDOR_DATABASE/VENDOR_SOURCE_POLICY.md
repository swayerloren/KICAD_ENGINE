# Vendor Source Policy

## Hard Rules

1. Use official vendor or distributor pages as source links, not as package-proof by default.
2. Do not store private pricing, credentials, or tokens here.
3. Vendor part numbers are identifiers, not approval evidence.
4. Supplier CAD models require separate package and mechanical verification.
5. Keep copied vendor page payloads out of canonical public-source-of-truth folders unless license permits.

## Preferred Use

- source link preservation
- part-number normalization
- vendor naming conventions
- sourcing cross-reference notes
- portal-specific warnings
