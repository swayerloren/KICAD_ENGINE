# Disclaimer

KiCad Engine is not official KiCad.

KiCad Engine is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or maintained by the KiCad project unless that relationship is explicitly established in the future by the relevant parties.

KiCad Engine is AI-assisted engineering support. It is not certified engineering approval, professional certification, safety certification, regulatory approval, or fabrication approval.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for final engineering and manufacturing decisions.

Users are responsible for final manufacturing decisions.

Before manufacturing or ordering boards, users must verify:

- ERC results.
- DRC results.
- BOM correctness.
- Footprint selection and dimensions.
- Symbols and symbol correctness.
- Pinouts.
- Datasheets and errata.
- Connector orientation and mating parts.
- Polarity-sensitive components.
- Mechanical fit.
- Board outline, mounting, and enclosure constraints.
- Gerbers.
- Drill files.
- Pick-and-place files.
- STEP and 3D model outputs.
- Fab outputs, fabrication notes, and assembly instructions.

## AI Limits

AI agents can misunderstand schematics, footprints, datasheets, KiCad files, and manufacturing outputs.

AI-generated reviews, scripts, summaries, and recommendations must be treated as assistance only. They do not replace qualified engineering review, vendor documentation, KiCad ERC/DRC checks, or manufacturer requirements.

## Datasheets And Component Records

Datasheet and component database entries may be incomplete, unverified, or link-only because redistribution rights vary by vendor and document.

Do not treat a datasheet summary, part record, symbol candidate, or footprint candidate as authoritative unless the source and verification status support that claim.

## Manufacturing

Manufacturing-style outputs generated by KiCad Engine must remain `NOT_FINAL` until the full verification gate passes and the user explicitly approves release.
