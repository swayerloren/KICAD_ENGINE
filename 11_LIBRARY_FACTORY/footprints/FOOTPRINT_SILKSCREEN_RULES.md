# Footprint Silkscreen Rules

## Purpose

Silkscreen should aid assembly and inspection without violating manufacturing rules.

## Rules

- Do not draw silkscreen over exposed pads.
- Mark pin 1 or polarity where useful.
- Keep reference designator readable when practical.
- Avoid silkscreen inside connector mating areas.
- Avoid silkscreen under components where it will be hidden and unhelpful.

## Polarity And Orientation

Use clear orientation marks for:

- Diodes.
- LEDs.
- Electrolytic capacitors.
- IC pin 1.
- Connectors where pin 1 matters.

## Review Gate

Silkscreen is not proof of footprint correctness. It must match the pad and fab-layer orientation.
