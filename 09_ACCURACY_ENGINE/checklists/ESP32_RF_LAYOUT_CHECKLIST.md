# ESP32 RF Layout Checklist

Rules:

- `09_ACCURACY_ENGINE/pcb_rules/ESP32_RF_ANTENNA_LAYOUT_RULES.md`
- `09_ACCURACY_ENGINE/pcb_rules/MOUNTING_HOLE_MECHANICAL_RULES.md`

## Checks

- Antenna end faces the free edge.
- Keepout contains no copper, vias, traces, or components.
- Mounting holes and connector shells stay clear of the RF zone.
- USB and buck activity stay out of the antenna corridor.
- Orientation proof exists for the module.
