## Checks

- Functional blocks are recognizable and grouped.
- Flow reads left-to-right or top-to-bottom.
- Local same-block connections use wires where clearer than labels.
- USB-C block is local and readable.
- Buck block is local and readable.
- ESP32 control and decoupling blocks are local and readable.
- References and values do not overlap wires, pins, or symbols.
- Review notes do not crowd active circuitry.
- No visible placeholder references remain.
- Visual exports and crop review exist.
