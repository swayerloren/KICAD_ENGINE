# USB-C Layout Checklist

Rules:

- `09_ACCURACY_ENGINE/pcb_rules/USB_C_LAYOUT_RULES.md`
- `09_ACCURACY_ENGINE/pcb_rules/USB_ESD_PLACEMENT_RULES.md`

## Checks

- Receptacle mouth faces off-board.
- `CC1` and `CC2` resistors sit near the connector.
- ESD sits near the connector entry.
- `D+` and `D-` stay short and paired.
- Shield/shell grounding policy is recorded.
- USB path avoids RF keepouts and obvious return-path splits.
