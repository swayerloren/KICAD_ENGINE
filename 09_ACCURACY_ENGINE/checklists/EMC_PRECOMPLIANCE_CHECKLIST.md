# EMC Precompliance Checklist

- [ ] RF keepout evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Switching/power loop regions reviewed.
- [ ] Return-path continuity reviewed.
- [ ] USB and connector cable-entry paths reviewed.
- [ ] Mounting and enclosure interaction reviewed where relevant.
- [ ] Any harsh-environment or high-reliability assumptions documented.
- [ ] Result recorded as risk screen only, not certification.
