# Courtyard And Clearance Rules

## Purpose

Keep placement and fabrication review honest.

## Rules

- Respect KiCad courtyard warnings.
- Treat courtyard overlap as a review item, not cosmetic noise.
- Check connector keepouts, board-edge overhang, and mating clearance.
- Check heatsink, button, switch, display, RF, and module keepouts.
- Do not silence DRC without recording the physical reason.

## Review Items

- Courtyard clearance.
- Mechanical enclosure clearance.
- Assembly tool access.
- Test point access.
- Connector mating envelope.
- RF antenna keepout.

## Exit Criteria

All courtyard/clearance violations must be resolved or documented as intentional with human review.
