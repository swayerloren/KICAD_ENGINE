## Common Mistakes

- Selecting a footprint by package name only.
- Copying Blue Pill circuits without source/license/variant review.
- Omitting BOOT0/BOOT1 recovery access.
- Repurposing SWD pins without a recovery plan.
- Treating `VERIFIED_FROM_KICAD_LIBRARY` as `VERIFIED_FROM_DATASHEET`.

## Suitable Use Cases

- `INFERRED_FROM_COMMON_DESIGN`: Legacy STM32F1 projects, educational designs, Blue Pill-compatible firmware experiments, and simple embedded control where STM32F103C8T6 is intentionally selected.
