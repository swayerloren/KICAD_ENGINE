# High-Risk Components Index

## High-Risk Families

- ESP32 modules and SoCs
- USB-C connectors
- barrel jacks and power-entry connectors
- PMOS reverse-polarity FETs
- switching regulators and inductors
- ESD and TVS arrays
- polarized capacitors
- test pads and mounting holes when mechanical interaction matters

## Required Proof Themes

- exact package or module drawing
- pad-number and symbol-pin mapping
- connector mating direction and board-edge orientation
- antenna keepout direction for RF modules
- verified land-pattern source
- human review when 3D/mechanical proof is missing
