# Power Review Checklist

## Required Evidence

- Input voltage range.
- Load current estimates.
- Regulator datasheets.
- Capacitor requirements.
- Thermal calculations or notes.
- Protection requirements.

## Review Steps

- Check every power rail source and load.
- Check regulator dropout, maximum input, output current, and thermal risk.
- Check capacitor value, voltage rating, ESR, and placement.
- Check high-current trace widths.
- Check fuse, TVS, reverse-polarity, and surge protection where needed.
- Check power-good, enable, and sequencing pins.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if load current, regulator limits, capacitor requirements, or input transient environment are unknown.
