# RF Review Checklist

RF layouts require source-backed layout and human review.

## Required Evidence

- Module or RF IC design guide.
- Antenna or connector datasheet.
- Stackup and impedance target.
- Keepout drawing.
- Matching network guidance.

## Review Steps

- Check antenna keepout.
- Check RF connector footprint and orientation.
- Check feedline width against stackup.
- Check ground stitching.
- Check matching components and DNP status.
- Check nearby metal, enclosure, cables, and mounting hardware.

## Stop Conditions

Stop if stackup, feedline geometry, antenna keepout, or RF connector drawing is unknown.
