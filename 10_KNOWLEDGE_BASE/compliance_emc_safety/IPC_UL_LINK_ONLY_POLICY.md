# IPC / UL Link-Only Policy

## Hard Rules

1. IPC, UL, and similar standards are link/summarize only unless redistribution is explicitly permitted.
2. Do not copy raw paid standard text into canonical public docs.
3. Quarantine copied standards-like source captures when license status is unclear.
4. Canonical rule docs should describe internal gating behavior, not reproduce restricted standard wording.

## Safe Canonical Alternatives

- source links
- internal review checklists
- internal pass/fail criteria
- evidence-routing notes
