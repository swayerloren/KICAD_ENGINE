# When To Stop And Ask A Human

Stop and ask the human when:

- The request requires editing KiCad project files and active project or backup is not confirmed.
- Connector orientation or mating cable direction is unknown.
- A footprint cannot be matched to an exact package drawing.
- A symbol pinout cannot be verified.
- Power current, voltage, thermal, or transient requirement is unknown.
- Automotive, RF, mains, battery safety, or high-current assumptions are unclear.
- The user asks for final manufacturing approval.
- ERC/DRC results contain unresolved issues.
- A package manager install, credential, or paid service would be needed.

## How To Ask

Ask a short, specific question with the decision needed and the risk of guessing.

Do not keep designing around an unknown that affects safety, manufacturing, or pinout correctness.
