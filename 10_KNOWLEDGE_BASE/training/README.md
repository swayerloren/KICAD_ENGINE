# Training Knowledge

Status: `LINK_FIRST_SUMMARY_LAYER`

This folder stores normalized training notes about how AI agents should use
course material, lectures, and tutorial-style sources without treating them as
engineering approval.

## Use

- Use training material for concepts, terminology, and review prompts.
- Prefer official KiCad, vendor, and datasheet sources before applying a
  training lesson to a design.
- Treat university or tutorial content as potentially stale on tooling,
  fabrication limits, and current part revisions.

## Source Confidence

- Official KiCad or manufacturer documents referenced by a training record may
  be promoted into higher-trust canonical folders.
- Training captures that remain mixed, index-like, or redistribution-risk stay
  in license quarantine or migration history.
