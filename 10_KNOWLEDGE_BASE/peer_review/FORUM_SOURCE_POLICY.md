# Forum Source Policy

Status: `ACTIVE_RULE`

## Allowed Uses

- Gather repeated failure patterns.
- Find examples of how engineers describe a bug.
- Build human-review checklists.
- Corroborate a concern already raised by a higher-trust source.

## Disallowed Uses

- Treating a single forum answer as design approval
- Treating a tag page or search result as evidence
- Treating a video channel page as footprint, routing, or EMC proof
- Treating community anecdotes as a replacement for datasheets or KiCad-native
  validation

## Confidence Model

- `LOW`: tag indexes, search pages, channel indexes, thread lists
- `LOW_TO_MEDIUM`: specific threads with concrete screenshots or debugging steps
- `MEDIUM`: repeated consensus across multiple independent discussions, still
  not source of truth

## Migration Rule

Raw captures from the drained peer-review and video-index intake folders stay in quarantine or history.
Only normalized policy and observation summaries belong here.
