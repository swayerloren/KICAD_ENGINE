# Peer Review Knowledge

Status: `LOW_CONFIDENCE_GUIDANCE`

This folder stores normalized notes about forum and peer-review material.

## Rule

Forum posts, Q&A threads, Reddit comments, and video discussion are guidance
only. They are useful for:

- spotting failure patterns,
- finding terminology,
- identifying common confusion points,
- collecting candidate review questions.

They are not authoritative proof for:

- footprint approval,
- connector orientation,
- RF keepout dimensions,
- DRC equivalence,
- manufacturing readiness.
