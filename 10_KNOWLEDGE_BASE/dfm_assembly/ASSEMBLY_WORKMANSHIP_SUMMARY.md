# Assembly Workmanship Summary

## Hard Points

- BOM/CPL format validation is not proof that assembly orientation is correct.
- Pick-and-place rotations require visual review against the PCB, silkscreen,
  and connector/polarity expectations.
- Pin 1, diode polarity, LED polarity, capacitor polarity, and connector mating
  direction must be reviewed before upload.
- Human review is still required even when vendor upload portals accept the
  files.

## Raw Source Handling

Copied JLCPCB, PCBWay, and other fab-house guidance pages were quarantined and
normalized into rules/checklists instead of being promoted directly.
