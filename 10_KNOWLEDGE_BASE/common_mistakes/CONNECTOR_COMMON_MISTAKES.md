# Connector Common Mistakes

## High-Risk Mistakes

- Selecting a footprint from pitch alone.
- Mirroring or rotating connector orientation incorrectly.
- Confusing top-entry, side-entry, vertical, horizontal, SMT, and through-hole variants.
- Ignoring mating connector or cable keying.
- Misreading pin 1 orientation.
- Forgetting mounting tabs, shell pads, or mechanical keepouts.
- Assuming generic connectors are approved parts.

## Agent Checks

- Require exact connector MPN where possible.
- Compare symbol pin numbers to drawing.
- Compare footprint pads to land pattern.
- Check 3D model if available.
- Require connector review checklist completion.

## Required Human Review

All external connectors require human orientation review before PCB approval.
