## Verification Status Values

- `MISSING`: Needed but not present.

- `REVISION_CHECKED`: Revision/date checked against source.
- `USED_FOR_REVIEW`: Used in a documented design review.
- `SUPERSEDED`: Kept for history but not preferred.
- `LEGACY_MIGRATION_PENDING`: Preserved from old folder layout and not yet curated.
