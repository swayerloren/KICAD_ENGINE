## Part-Number Rules For Agents

- Do not shorten orderable part numbers unless the BOM explicitly stores suffixes elsewhere.
- Do not assume suffix-to-package mapping without an official order-code table.
- Do not assume pin compatibility across package or memory-size variants.
- Do not treat module/dev-board markings as proof of genuine silicon.
- Mark every unknown as `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_SOURCE`.
