# RF Reference Design Checklist

## Source And License

- Source owner identified.
- License and redistribution status recorded.
- Stackup availability recorded.

## Technical Review

- RF module or IC identified.
- Antenna or connector exact MPN reviewed.
- Feedline impedance and stackup reviewed.
- Matching network reviewed.
- Antenna keepout reviewed.
- Ground stitching reviewed.
- Enclosure/cable/nearby metal implications reviewed.

## Reuse Warnings

- Do not copy feedline geometry without stackup.
- Do not copy matching values without tuning context.
- Do not copy antenna placement without keepout and enclosure review.

## Human Review Needed

- RF layout.
- Antenna keepout.
- Connector orientation.
- Stackup and impedance.
