# Automotive Reference Design Checklist

## Source And License

- Source owner identified.
- License and redistribution status recorded.
- Safety or standards scope recorded if present.

## Technical Review

- Input voltage and transient assumptions reviewed.
- Fuse and reverse-polarity approach reviewed.
- TVS/surge protection reviewed.
- Regulator maximum input and thermal behavior reviewed.
- CAN/LIN protection reviewed.
- Connector and harness pinout reviewed.
- Ground/chassis strategy reviewed.

## Reuse Warnings

- Do not copy protection values without target transient requirements.
- Do not copy harness pinouts without vehicle documentation.
- Do not imply automotive certification from a reference design.

## Human Review Needed

- Safety and fault assumptions.
- Protection sizing.
- Connector/harness orientation.
- Final manufacturing and vehicle-use decision.
