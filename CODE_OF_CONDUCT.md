# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on technical issues and evidence.
- Assume designs can have real-world safety and cost consequences.
- Be clear when something is unverified.
- Avoid pressuring maintainers or users to fabricate, manufacture, or ship designs without proper review.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Submitting secrets or credentials.
- Intentionally adding unsafe scripts.
- Falsely claiming design, datasheet, footprint, or fabrication verification.
- Encouraging users to bypass ERC, DRC, BOM, footprint, datasheet, connector, mechanical, or visual review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, scripts, and release channels.
