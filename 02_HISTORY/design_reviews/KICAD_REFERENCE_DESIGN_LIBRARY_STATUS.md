## Policy

- Link-only records are the default.
- Official vendor reference designs and compatible open hardware projects are preferred.
- Copied files require license, source, attribution, redistribution, and public-release review.
- Reference designs are evidence, not automatic approval.
- Agents must record what can be learned and what must not be copied.
