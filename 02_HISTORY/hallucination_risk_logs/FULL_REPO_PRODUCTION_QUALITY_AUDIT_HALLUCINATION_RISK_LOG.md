# Hallucination Risk Log - Full Repo Production Quality Audit

Date: `2026-05-03`

Risk level: `MEDIUM`

## Risk Sources

- Readiness scores involve engineering judgment, not formal benchmark results.
- Placeholder/weak/broken-reference scans are heuristic and may include false positives.
- Security/legal conclusions are practical risk notes, not legal determinations.
- Some generated project reports could be misread as completed PCB evidence unless their `BLOCKED` or `NOT_RUN` status is read.

## Mitigations

- Claims cite generated CSVs, JSON summaries, command output, or specific project reports.
- Unverified component/datasheet/footprint data was not upgraded to verified status.
- Public release classification was kept conservative.
- No KiCad design files or manufacturing outputs were edited/generated.

## Required Human Review

Human review is required before any public release, legal claim, security clearance, component verification claim, or fabrication-readiness claim.
