# RF Antenna Placement Rules

## Purpose

Define placement rules for onboard antennas, RF modules, and nearby components.

## Rules

- Place the RF module and establish its keepout before placing nearby parts.
- Do not place copper-bearing parts, connectors, holes, or tall parts in the antenna keepout.
- Preserve intended edge placement for onboard antenna modules where required.
- Keep noisy power or switcher parts away from the antenna area.

## Failure Conditions

- blocked antenna keepout
- connector or hole intrusion into keepout
- component grouping that forces later routing through the keepout area
