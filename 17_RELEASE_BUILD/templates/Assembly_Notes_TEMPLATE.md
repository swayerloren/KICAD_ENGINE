## General Notes

- Assemble according to uploaded BOM, Gerbers, and pick-and-place / CPL / centroid file.
- Verify all component orientation before production.
- Contact customer before making substitutions.

## Critical Orientation Notes

### Barrel Jack

- Confirm barrel jack female opening faces the correct off-board edge.
- Confirm rear solder pins/lugs match the actual footprint.
- Do not rotate based only on automated placement data.

### USB-C Connector

- Confirm USB-C receptacle mouth faces the board edge.
- Confirm shield tabs and fine-pitch pins align with footprint.

### ICs

- Confirm pin 1 orientation on all ICs/modules.

### Diodes / LEDs

- Confirm cathode/anode orientation.

### Polarized Capacitors

- Confirm polarity before assembly.
