# Safety And Limitations

Status: `PUBLIC_DRAFT`

## Not Official KiCad

KiCad Engine is not official KiCad and is not affiliated with KiCad unless separately documented.

## AI Limits

AI can help inspect, plan, summarize, and automate guarded workflows. It can also make mistakes. It must not be treated as certified engineering approval.

## Required Human Review

Users must verify:

- ERC and DRC results.
- BOM.
- Symbols.
- Footprints.
- Pinouts.
- Datasheets.
- Connector orientation.
- Polarity.
- Mechanical fit.
- Fab outputs.

## Manufacturing Outputs

All manufacturing-style outputs are `NOT_FINAL` until the full review gate passes.
