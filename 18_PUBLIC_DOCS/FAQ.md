# FAQ

Status: `PUBLIC_DRAFT`

## Is KiCad Engine KiCad?

No. It is not official KiCad and does not replace KiCad. It helps AI agents work around your installed KiCad app.

## Does it require paid AI tools?

No. It can be used with Codex, Claude, or similar VS Code-based agents, but users must provide their own accounts if those tools require login.

## Does it upload my PCB files?

The repo is designed for local-first work. Any AI tool you choose may have its own data handling rules, so review that tool's policy before sharing project details.

## Are datasheets bundled?

Usually no. Datasheets and vendor documents should be link-only unless redistribution rights are confirmed.

## Can AI approve fabrication?

No. AI review is not fabrication approval. Human review is required.
