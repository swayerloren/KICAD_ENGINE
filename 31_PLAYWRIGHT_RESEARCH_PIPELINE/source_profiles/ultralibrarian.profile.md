# Ultra Librarian Source Profile

Source confidence level: `PUBLIC_LIBRARY_VENDOR`

## Source Purpose

EDA model availability discovery and source-link evidence.

## Preferred Access Method

Public page link capture and manual review. Avoid automated downloads.

## Login/API Key Required

Downloads may require account or acceptance. Do not automate logged-in access.

## Playwright Allowed

Allowed for public-page evidence only.

## Fields May Be Captured

- public model page URL
- part number
- package text
- symbol/footprint/model availability text

## Must Not Be Captured

- downloaded model files without license review
- logged-in account pages
- terms-gated content
- raw page archives

## Rate Limit Guidance

Low volume only.

## Redistribution Guidance

Link-only unless redistribution is confirmed.

## Notes For Codex/Claude

Vendor/library model availability is not proof the KiCad footprint is correct.
