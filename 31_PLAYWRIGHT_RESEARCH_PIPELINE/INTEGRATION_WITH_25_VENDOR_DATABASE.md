# Integration With 25_VENDOR_DATABASE

Playwright research may add link-first vendor portal notes and source profiles.

## Allowed Outputs

- Vendor source URLs.
- Official documentation portal links.
- Product page URLs.
- Lifecycle page links.
- Public source confidence notes.

## Rules

- Do not store account credentials.
- Do not scrape vendor portals.
- Do not copy restricted vendor documents.
- Mark lifecycle and availability data as time-sensitive.
